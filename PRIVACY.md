# ExpiryWatch privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

ExpiryWatch is a free app that keeps track of when your items expire. It has no account, no ads
and no analytics. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Data stored on your device

The items you add, your categories and storage locations, the products you've scanned before and
your settings are stored only on your device. ExpiryWatch has no server and doesn't collect them.

- **Android backup**: if backup is on in your device's settings, Android may include ExpiryWatch's
  items and settings in your device backup, which Google stores encrypted in your Google account.
  You control this in the system settings.
- **Backup files**: when you export a backup, it's saved where you choose, and read only when you
  import it.
- **Diagnostics log**: if you turn on Record diagnostics, a log of what the app does is kept on your
  device. It contains no item names. It leaves the device only if you export and share it.

## Data sent to others

- **Open Food Facts**: when you scan a barcode of a product the app doesn't know yet, the barcode
  number is sent to Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product's
  name, brand and photo, and product photos are loaded from there. Open Food Facts can see your IP
  address, as any website can. You can turn this off in Settings → Advanced options → Look up
  products online. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy:
  https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
- **Google ML Kit** (Google Play version): barcodes are recognized on your device; camera images
  are never stored or sent. Google's ML Kit may send Google anonymous information about how it
  performs, as described in its terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
- **Tips through Google Play** (Google Play version): if you choose to leave a tip, Google Play
  handles the payment under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  ExpiryWatch never sees your payment details.
- **PayPal** (versions from outside Google Play): if you choose to donate, the app opens PayPal,
  which handles it under its own privacy policy. ExpiryWatch never sees your payment details.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: only to scan barcodes, and only while the scanner is open.
- **Notifications**: to show the daily reminder.
- **Alarms & reminders** (optional): to show the reminder at the exact time you chose.
- **Run at startup**: to set the reminder again after your device restarts.
- **Internet**: for the product lookup and photos described above, and for tips.

## Children

ExpiryWatch isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.
