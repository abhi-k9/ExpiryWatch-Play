ExpiryWatch keeps track of when your groceries, medicine and anything else expires, and reminds you before things go off, so less ends up in the bin.

It's free, has no ads, and every feature will stay free.

<b>Add items in seconds</b>
• Scan a barcode and the name, brand and photo are filled in for you
• Products you've saved before are remembered, so the next time is even quicker
• Several of the same product? Add each expiry date at once, such as for every carton of a multipack
• Record when you opened something and how long it keeps afterwards; the earlier date counts

<b>Always know what to use first</b>
• Items are sorted by expiry and grouped into Expired, Expiring soon and Fresh
• Several items of the same product fold into one row that shows the next to expire
• Search, and filter by category or storage location
• Swipe right when something is used up, left when it's thrown away, with undo

<b>Reminders that fit your day</b>
• A daily reminder at the time you choose, listing what has expired or is about to
• Optionally at the exact time, even when your phone is idle
• A home-screen widget showing what expires next

<b>See how you're doing</b>
• Insights show how much you used up versus threw away, a monthly trend and the categories you waste most

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• No account and no ads; everything is stored on your device
• Back up to and restore from a file you choose
• Looking up products online can be turned off, so barcodes never leave your device

ExpiryWatch is open source: https://github.com/abhi-k9/ExpiryWatch-Play

Product data comes from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License.
